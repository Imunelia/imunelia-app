Development and quality

Science without shortcuts

This public section explains how Immunalia approaches development, quality and information. It does not make claims about treating or preventing disease or about changing specific biological markers.

Raw-material quality

Development considers the identity, origin, specification, purity and documentation of raw materials. The objective is a consistent product and traceability across individual batches.

Technological parameters

Physical properties, stability, sensitivity to moisture, mechanical integrity and packaging suitability are assessed. These parameters describe product quality and processing, not a therapeutic effect.

Working with evidence

Laboratory, observational and clinical information have different levels of evidential value. Findings concerning an individual substance, model, marker or isolated observation cannot automatically be transferred to a finished product.

Responsible communication

Public communication does not use biomarkers, biological mechanisms, case reports or changes in health status as proof of product effect. It provides only information appropriate to a food supplement and the applicable legal framework.

Separate layers

Development, documentation and product communication are not the same.

Internal development may work with technical materials and professional questions. Such materials do not in themselves constitute claims about the effect of a finished product and are not a basis for public treatment or prevention promises.

What this page is

An overview of Immunalia's approach to quality, technological processing, stability, manufacturing documentation and careful interpretation of information.

What this page is not

It is not medical advice, a description of a mechanism of action, clinical proof or a promise of diagnostic, therapeutic or preventive activity.
