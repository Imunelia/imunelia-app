Development and quality

Science without shortcuts

This public section summarises our approach to raw-material selection, technological processing, stability, packaging and product documentation.

Raw materials and specifications

We review the origin, identity, specification and documentation of the raw materials used. Traceability and consistent batch quality are central to this process.

Manufacturing consistency

The manufacturing process is designed to support repeatability, processing accuracy and conformity of the finished product with its defined specification.

Stability and packaging

We assess the effects of time, moisture, storage and packaging on product properties. Packaging forms part of the overall protection and preservation of quality.

Transparent information

Public content is based on the final product specification and uses clear, accurate and proportionate language without unsupported conclusions.

Each type of information has its place

Technical documentation and public communication serve different purposes.

Technical data support development, quality management and product documentation. The public website uses only information consistent with the final specification and the rules applicable to the relevant product category.

Documentation

Development documentation includes supplier records, raw-material specifications, manufacturing records, packaging information and storage conditions.

Regular review

Public website content is reviewed on an ongoing basis so that it remains factual, current and aligned with the final form of the products.
