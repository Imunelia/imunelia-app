Quality and Development

From raw material to finished product.

Immunalia development is built around defined raw materials, a repeatable manufacturing process, suitable packaging and documentation of each important step.

Raw materials and specifications

We review the origin, identity, specification and documentation of the raw materials used. Traceability and consistent batch quality are central to this process.

Product development

Formulation, technological processing and packaging are evaluated as one system. The objective is a stable and reproducible final product.

Manufacturing accuracy

The manufacturing process is designed to support repeatability, processing accuracy and conformity of the finished product with its defined specification.

Stability and packaging

We assess the effects of time, moisture, storage and packaging on product properties. Packaging forms part of the overall protection and preservation of quality.

Quality management

Each batch must meet defined requirements.

Technical documentation includes supplier records, raw-material specifications, manufacturing records, packaging information, storage conditions and finished-product checks.

Batch documentation

Manufacturing and control records support traceability of raw materials, key processing steps and the final specification.

Responsible communication

Public information is based on the final product specification and uses clear, factual and proportionate language without unsupported conclusions.
